Two shared root causes of recoverable headroom:
  (A) 64-bit-word / large-state hashes (SHA3/Keccak, Whirlpool, BLAKE2b, SHA512/384, Streebog):
      state doesn't fit in registers -> 236-255 reg -> occupancy collapses to ~16% -> latency
      stalls (low IPC). Lever: cut register pressure / spills, raise occupancy.
  (B) RC4-based modes (Kerberos etype23 roasting, old Office, PDF): data-dependent 256-byte
      S-box swaps -> latency-bound (~69% compute, IPC ~1.5, 21% occ), neither pipe saturated.
      Lever: S-box placement, ILP, invariant-HMAC precompute.

For contrast, the ENTIRE 32-bit MD/SHA1/SHA2-256/NTLM family measures 92-98% ALU AND is
instruction-efficient (measured ops/hash at or below full-hash minimum via early-reject) =
at the roofline, no recoverable headroom. NTLM validated at ~73 int ops/candidate vs ~80 floor.
